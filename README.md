For a genuine Private Seller transaction, there is generally no automatic statutory right to return an Item simply because the Buyer changed their mind. The Item must still be accurately described and the Seller must have the right to sell it.
A Seller may voluntarily offer a returns policy that is more generous.
6.3 Business Seller purchases
A consumer buying from a Business Seller has statutory rights that cannot be removed by OurCatch's policies.
